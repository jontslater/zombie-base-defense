# Assets

All shipped art for Milestone 1 is **placeholder pixel art** generated in-repo (see commit history for `assets/sprites/*.png`). It is dedicated to the project and released under **CC0 1.0**.

## Kenney (CC0) — reference packs for future art passes

These Kenney.nl packs are **not copied into this repo** for M1; they are the preferred source when replacing placeholders with production sprites.

| Base / turrets | Tiny Town | https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town | CC0 1.0 |
